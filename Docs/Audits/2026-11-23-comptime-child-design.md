### Ranked Bottlenecks Inside `comptime` & Re-Finalize (Biggest Wins First)
1. **Batch compile + ELF bind + `X64Patch` (estimated `~35–45 ms` on `RunColdContext` by subtraction `68.14 - 9.09 - 3.63 - 5.37 - 0.08`, `~5–6 s` across self-host — S1 adds 5 `trace_sink` spans in `Native.jet` to confirm exact sub-phase splits before landing S2/S3):**
   - `Native.jet:547–594` runs hand-written `X64Patch` (`~2.5×` vs `>= 10×` under `#6795 c2`), then `DirectBind.jet:23–310` constructs full ELF phdrs/shdrs/`.symtab`/`.strtab`, `__jet_bind_main`, dummy `__jet_comptime_none` (`Native.jet:511–519`), and `.eh_frame` (`DirectBind.jet:139–141, 156–158, 216–224, 284`) to match `x64_bind_runtime_image_at` ELF parity (`DirectBind.jet:6–7`), and `Native.jet:568–584` immediately re-parses that ELF.
   - `Native.jet:138–139, 706–724` (`defined_names`/`defined_hits`) only caches `0`/`1`/`2` existence booleans, not symbol addresses, forcing repeated `link_image_view` parses on every batch.
2. **`comptime.land.splice` double owner-module check (`1,282.3 ms` on self-host):**
   - `LocalSplice.jet:979–1033` already restricts work strictly to `owner_modules` that contain matching body sites (`BodyComptime.jet:701–707` exits immediately when `pending.len() == 0`). The real `1.28 s` cost is `LocalSplice.jet:1051–1059` running `sema_finalize_check_modules` **twice** per owner module: Pass 1 (`earlier_graph`, line `1051`) solely to reconstruct baseline `before`/`earlier_demand` for refusal checks, and Pass 2 (`pass_graph`, line `1056`) to produce the spliced `after` bodies.
3. **Per-item IPC round-trips, `ALLOC` leak & value readback (`Native.jet:842, 858–908`):**
   - Every single native item call currently executes: (a) 1 `X64_COMPTIME_ALLOC` round-trip (`Native.jet:869`, never freed in child), (b) 1 `X64_COMPTIME_CALL` round-trip (`Native.jet:876–880`, where `ComptimeServe.jet:147` returns `result` in `DONE` word 1 and `Native.jet:908` discards it!), (c) 1 `poll` syscall in `x64_comptime_settle` (`Native.jet:842`) on every individual item, and (d) `process_vm_readv` syscalls (`Native.jet:891–893, 1475`) after `jet_eval_native_forget` (`Native.jet:906, 1399`) cleared `s.pages`.
4. **`comptime.precheck` (`2.93 ms` = `51%` of `JetcTrace`, `9.09 ms` on `RunColdContext`, `531 ms` on self-host) & `comptime.prefetch` (`642 ms` on self-host).**
5. **Per-compile entry stub (`Native.jet:341–365, 410–435`):** 2 functions / 9 IR ops compiled & bound once per session; small compared to (1)–(4), so gated on measured sub-phase timing (`>= 2 ms`) before touching `Compiler/build.jet`.

## 2. Strongest Existing Implementations (Fair Best Case & Trade-Offs)

| System & Primary Source | Best-Case Mechanism | What It Does That Jet Cannot Copy Naively (Trade-Off) |
|---|---|---|
| **Zig `comptime`** (`src/Sema.zig`, `src/InternPool.zig` — ziglang/zig) | In-process bytecode/AST interpreter over `InternPool` (zero IPC, zero spawn, zero readback). | Violates Jet's `D-EXEC2=A` and `I9` (no second interpreter; compile-time code runs natively at `-O0` on the one shared runtime with OS-enforced seccomp isolation). |
| **rustc CTFE / Miri** (`compiler/rustc_const_eval/src/interpret/`) | In-process MIR interpreter with deterministic memory model and zero OS boundary. | Same `D-EXEC2=A`/`I9` constraint; second semantic engine risks drift and loses native execution speed. |
| **Copy-and-Patch (`#6795`)** (Xu & Kjolstad, OOPSLA 2021; CPython PEP 744) | Native binary-stencil emission directly into executable memory (`RW → RX`) with direct relocation patching (zero ELF `.o` serialization). | Runs in-process without seccomp; Jet preserves the isolated warm child (`SessionHost.jet:54`) while emitting batches directly without ELF synthesis and loading into page-disjoint `R`/`RX`/`RW` memory via `comptime_serve_shm_load`. |
| **Linux `memfd` + `MAP_SHARED` batched IPC** (Wayland shm / Mojo) | Sealed `memfd` (`F_SEAL_SHRINK | F_SEAL_GROW`) mapped in both processes; batched RPC executes `N` items in 1 round-trip and packs return values into a bounds-checked shared window. | Requires strict TOCTOU protection, single-load header validation, overflow-safe bounds checks, and recursion/size caps against an untrusted child (`I1`). |

## 3. Runnable Probe (IPC Round-Trips & Value Transport: `ALLOC+CALL+poll+vm_readv` vs Scalar `DONE` vs `CALL_BATCH+MAP_SHARED`)

We built and executed an ephemeral two-process Linux fork/pipe/`MAP_SHARED`/`process_vm_readv` microbenchmark (compiled via `jet cc -O3` in scratch, not a checked-in fixture) executing `N = 500` native comptime items (matching a realistic native batch count, distinct from pure sema-folded `comptime.apply` counts):

| IPC + Readback Path (`N = 500` native items) | Total Wall | Latency / Item | Speedup vs Current |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Path A (Current):** per-item `ALLOC` + `CALL` + `poll` + `process_vm_readv` | `5.315 ms` | `10.63 µs/item` | `1.0×` |
| **Path B:** direct scalar return in `DONE` word 1 (no `ALLOC`, no `vm_readv`) | `2.744 ms` | `5.49 µs/item` | **`1.94×`** |
| **Path C:** single `CALL_BATCH` (1 `poll` per wave) returning `N` values packed into `MAP_SHARED` | **`0.008 ms`** | **`0.015 µs/item`** | **`706.3×`** |

*(Prior ephemeral 20k × 64 B pure-readback microprobe also confirmed `MAP_SHARED` reads at `1.2 ns/val` vs `11.2 ns/val` page-batched `process_vm_readv` and `571.4 ns/val` scattered `process_vm_readv` — `9.3×` to `475×` faster.)*

## 4. Target Architecture (`c2`–`c4`)

### 4.1 Direct Zero-ELF Batch Loader, Sub-Phase Spans & Cached Image Symbol Addresses (Independent of `#6795 c2`)
- **Instrument `Native.jet` sub-phases first (S1 entry):** Add 5 lightweight `trace_sink` spans (`comptime.ir_build`, `comptime.x64_compile`, `comptime.batch_bind`, `comptime.child_spawn`, `comptime.child_call`) to confirm exact wall shares before landing S2/S3.
- **Upgrade `JetEvalNativeImage` (`Native.jet:129–140`):** Replace existence-only `defined_hits` (`0`/`1`/`2`) with cached symbol addresses `defined_addr: [Int]` populated once from `.jet.exports` + `.symtab` (same table as `DirectBind.jet:329–343`, unified with `jet_eval_native_missing`), eliminating repeated `link_image_view` parses on every batch!
- **Preserve `x64_bind_functions_at` ELF parity (`DirectBind.jet:6–7, 139–141, 216–224, 284`):** Keep `x64_bind_functions_at` untouched where ELF parity with `x64_bind_runtime_image_at` is required, and introduce a dedicated **zero-ELF comptime batch loader** (`x64_bind_comptime_batch` -> `X64ComptimeCode`) in `DirectBind.jet` + `Native.jet:547–594` that:
  - Omits ELF phdrs/shdrs/`.symtab`/`.strtab`, `__jet_bind_main`, dummy `__jet_comptime_none` (`Native.jet:511–519`), and unused `dwarf_eh_frame` (Jet's comptime child has no `__register_frame_info`).
  - Lays out `.text` (`RX`), `.rodata` (`R`, respecting `x64_object_read_only` relocation rules at `DirectBind.jet:99–135, 269–276`), and writable `.data` (`RW`) directly at page-disjoint virtual addresses starting at `permit.value.base`, enforcing `link_fits32` checks (`DirectBind.jet:244`) and patching code + 64-bit data relocations directly against `defined_addr` + batch-local section bases.
  - Stages the laid-out segments into `MAP_SHARED` (`MFD_NOEXEC_SEAL`, respecting W^X at `Loader.jet:379–387`) and maps them into page-disjoint anonymous `R`/`RX`/`RW` pages in the child via existing `comptime_serve_shm_load` (`ComptimeServe.jet:240–260`).
  - Populates `X64ComptimeCode` (`segments`, `bytes`, `high`) for child respawn reload (`Native.jet:825–838`), sorted `program.functions` + `program.function_names` for E3012 stack-overflow naming (`Native.jet:1047–1053`), and `program.listing` directly from `IRX64Program` without re-parsing ELF!

### 4.2 Sound, Zero-`#Unsafe`-Growth `MAP_SHARED` Value Transport & `CALL_BATCH` (`F1`, `F2`, `R1`, `R3`, `R4` Resolved)
Rather than diverting `Alloc.jet` (which would break 64 KiB `ALLOC_PAGE_MAP`, `rt_text_copy`/`int_new`, stop-frame UAF, and temporaries) or expecting dumb `ComptimeServe.jet` to know Jet layouts, use a **three-tier sound transport**:

1. **Tier 1 — Scalar fast path in `DONE` word 1 (zero `ALLOC`, zero `MAP_SHARED`, zero `vm_readv`):**
   - For non-`loads` wrappers (keeping the `loads == true` debugger path at `Native.jet:623–665` unchanged!) returning scalar shapes (`Int`, `Bool`, `Char`, `Float`, `Float32`, `Word`, `Unit`), emit `jet_eval_native_wrapper` returning the scalar word directly in `rax` (dropping the unused `cell` parameter). `ComptimeServe.jet:147` sends that word in `DONE` word 1 and `Native.jet:908` decodes it directly — skipping `X64_COMPTIME_ALLOC` (and its child-heap leak) and `process_vm_readv`:
     - **`Float` / `Float32` ABI:** The wrapper explicitly bit-casts `xmm0 -> rax` (returning `.Word` bit pattern) so `coll_call5` (`ComptimeServe.jet:136`) returns the exact IEEE-754 bits in `rax`; `Float32` masks `word & 4294967295`.
     - **Narrow scalars:** The wrapper zero-/sign-extends (or parent masks on decode matching `Native.jet:1653, 1720–1735`: `word & 255 != 0` for `Bool`, exact width masking/sign-extension for 1/2/4-byte `.Word` / `Char`).
     - **`.Int` inline vs boxed BigInt (`Int.jet:29–40`, `Native.jet:1668–1689`):** Return the raw `Int` word in `DONE` word 1 (zero `ALLOC`); parent checks `jet_eval_ir_inline(word)` — inline `Int` decodes in zero syscalls, while bit-62 boxed BigInt falls back to limb read (`process_vm_readv` / Tier 3).
   - **Strict `stderr` refusal preservation (`R1`):** Because `ComptimeWire.jet:206–207` always permits `write` on `fd 2` (`stderr`) for runtime stops, **never** skip `child.errors` draining on `!s.runtime_execution`! Single-item calls still run `x64_comptime_settle(s.child)` (`Native.jet:842–854`); `CALL_BATCH` waves perform **one** `x64_comptime_settle(s.child)` after the batch — if `child.errors` is non-empty on a sealed batch, the parent falls back to replaying the wave per-item to attribute the exact offending item (`jet_eval_ir_effect_refused(s, "standard error", false)`), preserving 100% refusal semantics with 1 `poll` per wave instead of `N`!
2. **Tier 2 — Direct parent `.rodata` resolution (zero syscalls, zero child copy):**
   - When decoding a `String`/`Bytes` pointer inside `[batch_rodata_lo, batch_rodata_hi)` or `jet_runtime.image`'s `.rodata`, read the static literal bytes directly from parent-owned `program.code.bytes` / `JetEvalNativeImage` (zero IPC, zero `process_vm_readv`).
3. **Tier 3 — Scoped wrapper packing into a resettable `MAP_SHARED` return window + `CALL_BATCH`:**
   - **Scoped packer (avoiding IR bloat & `Values.jet` duplication, `R3`):** Restrict wrapper-emitted Tier 3 packing to hot flat/shallow value shapes (`String`, `[U8]`, flat structs/enums/lists of scalars and strings) writing **only the returned value graph** (never temporaries, globals, `#Memo`, or stopped frames -> **100% UAF-free on per-item cursor reset!**) into a dedicated `MAP_SHARED` return window (`shm_ret`, offset-encoded relative to `shm_ret_base`). Rare/complex shapes (`Err` metadata graphs, hashed/static maps, `JetFraction`/`JetDecimal`/`JetAuthority`, recursive graphs) or values exceeding `shm_ret` set a fallback tag and decode transparently via `process_vm_readv` in `Values.jet:115–250`!
   - **`CALL_BATCH` wave semantics (`R3`):** Batch waves only group `>= 2` independent items sharing the same `#StepLimit(n)` fuel/admission mode and statically free of `jet_comptime_host*` callbacks (items using `HOST` callbacks execute on the single-item `CALL` path). Inside a `CALL_BATCH` wave:
     - **Per-item fuel:** Each item resets/enforces its own step budget before invocation so item `k` cannot silently starve item `k+1`.
     - **`STOP` / `REFUSED` / crash / overflow at item `k`:** The child aborts items `k+1..N` and reports `(completed = k, status_k)` (or dies on `STOP`/`SIGSYS`); parent decodes completed items `0..<k` (re-checking `child.errors`), records item `k`'s stop/refusal, respawns the warm child if needed (`Native.jet:825–838`), and executes remaining items `k+1..N` cleanly.
     - **`stderr` check:** One `x64_comptime_settle` after the batch; if `child.errors` is non-empty on a sealed batch, replay per-item for exact refusal attribution.
4. **Hardening, Trust Boundary & Zero Net `#Unsafe` Growth (`I1`, `UnsafeRatchet <= 1654`, `R3`/`R4`):**
   - **Seal the `memfd`:** Pass `MFD_CLOEXEC | MFD_NOEXEC_SEAL | MFD_ALLOW_SEALING` (`flags = 11`, with `flags = 3` fallback if unsupported) via existing `os_memfd_create_i64` (`Linux.jet:456`) and apply `fcntl(F_ADD_SEALS, F_SEAL_SHRINK | F_SEAL_GROW)` via existing `os_fcntl_i64` (`Linux.jet:372`) in `SessionHost.jet:471–492` before spawning so a hostile/buggy child can never truncate `memfd` and raise `SIGBUS` in the parent (adds `0` `#Unsafe` regions).
   - **Unconditional parent memory safety vs `SHM_QUIESCENT` (`R3`):** **Parent memory safety (`I1`) NEVER depends on `SHM_QUIESCENT` (which is child-emitted and untrusted).** Unconditional parent safety is guaranteed solely by: sealed `memfd` (`F_SEAL_SHRINK | F_SEAL_GROW`), loading every header/length/offset word **once** into a local `Int`, overflow-safe bounds checks (`offset >= 0 && count >= 0 && offset <= shm_len && count <= shm_len - offset`), recursion-depth cap (`<= 64`), total decoded byte cap (`<= JET_EVAL_NATIVE_READ_MAX`), and copying leaf bytes into owned `TComptimeValue.String`/`Bytes` *before* UTF-8 validation (never borrowing `MAP_SHARED`!). Separately, `SHM_QUIESCENT` (checked in `ComptimeServe.jet` via `thread_tls_registry` `live.len() == 2 && THREAD_SPAWNING == 0` at `Thread.jet:58–66, 73`) acts strictly as a **benign-child determinism guard** against torn reads from live Jet worker threads (triggering `process_vm_readv` fallback when non-quiescent).
   - **Zero net `#Unsafe` growth (`UnsafeRatchet <= 1654`, `0` outside kernel, `R4`):** Safe bounds-checked readers `x64_comptime_shm_read_word` and `x64_comptime_shm_read_bytes` live inside `Compiler/JetBackend/Source/Image/SessionHost.jet` (already in `JetBackend`'s declared memory kernel) calling `OS/Linux.jet`'s `os_load_word`/`os_load_byte` (`Linux.jet:526–538`). To keep total lexical `#Unsafe` regions strictly `<= 1654` (`UnsafeRatchet.jet:4–8`), the same S2 patch consolidates existing adjacent `#Unsafe` blocks in `SessionHost.jet` (lines `340`+`345`, `415`+`420`, and folding `x64_comptime_take`'s caller guards at `324`/`373` into `x64_comptime_take` itself) — **0 `#Unsafe` in `JetEval`, 0 outside kernel, and `<= 0` net `#Unsafe` delta!**

### 4.3 Warm Child Lifecycle, Optional Digest-Keyed Entry Stub & Sound `LocalSplice` Speedup (`R2`)
- **One warm child per compile (`#6798 c2` & `#6794 S5` alignment):** Keep one warm `sealed` child per compile (reaped cleanly at `jet_eval_native_session_close`, `Native.jet:400–406`, respawned only after a fatal stop/refusal at `Native.jet:825–838`; `runtime` child spawns only for `fn build`/`fn dev`/`jet eval` that admit stdout). Reconciled with `#6794 S5`: default is strictly 1 warm child per compile per `#6798 c2`, proved by `Comptime/one_warm_child_per_compile`; `#6794 S5` only fans out past 1 if its DAG-width probe proves multi-item wall savings and stays aligned with `#6798 c2`.
- **Entry stub (`Native.jet:341–365`, gated on measurement):** Profile stub compile+bind time first on `RunColdContext`/`JetcTrace` via S1's `trace_sink` spans. Only if it exceeds `>= 2 ms`, embed a **digest-keyed** pre-bound stub template (`tail`, `tail_at`, `image_end`, `patch_at`, `patch_bytes`, `image_symbols_left` keyed on `jet_runtime.image` SHA-256) + pre-compiled `.o` fallback, dynamically attaching `image_path`/`image_stamp`/`image_at` from `JetEvalNativeImage` at spawn time and falling back to binding the `.o` whenever the session image digest differs (preserving `jet_cli_core_check_image` at `Request.jet:344–361` and `jet-bare`!).
- **Sound `LocalSplice` & `comptime.precheck` cuts (`R2`, zero default-build soundness bypass):**
  - Profile and eliminate redundant work in `comptime.precheck` (`2.93 ms` = `51%` of `JetcTrace`, `9.09 ms` on `RunColdContext`) and `comptime.prefetch` (`642 ms` on self-host).
  - In `LocalSplice.jet:1051–1134` (`1,282 ms` on self-host): Pass 1 (`earlier_graph`, line `1051`) computes baseline `before`/`earlier_demand` solely to drive mandatory splice-refusal invariants (`same_named` at `1071`, `same_demand` at `1072`, `sema_module_body_rows_difference` at `653`, `inner_call` at `676`, `finalized_bodies` at `693`, `dropped` diagnostics at `535, 1132`), while Pass 2 (`pass_graph`, line `1056`) produces the `after` bodies actually spliced at `1096–1134`. We **never** skip Pass 2 and **never** gate mandatory refusal checks off behind `!request.recheck_verify`. Instead, S3 profiles `sema.local_values` (Pass 1 vs Pass 2) vs `.compare` vs `.splice`, and eliminates redundant Pass 1 re-checking **soundly** by capturing and reusing the already-computed baseline rows and demand snapshot from the initial owner-module check — preserving **every** refusal invariant in default builds and verifying byte-identical behavior with `JET_RECHECK_VERIFY=1`!
