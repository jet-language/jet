# E0359: `+` can't combine Length with Time

**Code:** `E0359`

## What

`+` can't combine Length with Time

## Why

physical quantities must have compatible dimensions before they can be added, subtracted, or compared

## Fix

use matching dimensions, or use `*` or `/` to derive a new dimension
