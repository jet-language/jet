# E0109: `+` can't compare or combine U8 (an 8-bit whole number, 0 to 255) and I8 (an 8-bit whole number, -128 to 127)

**Code:** `E0109`

## What

`+` can't compare or combine U8 (an 8-bit whole number, 0 to 255) and I8 (an 8-bit whole number, -128 to 127)

## Why

neither U8 contains every value of I8, nor I8 every value of U8

## Fix

choose the result type and convert the other side into it

Failing program: [`tests/ui/mixed_numbers.jet`](../../../tests/ui/mixed_numbers.jet)
