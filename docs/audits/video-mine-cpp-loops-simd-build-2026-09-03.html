What comes next

The two-program rule makes speed claims earn trust.

A speed feature must beat the plain spelling it replaces before shipping.

  1. What shape does every tier run?

    Structured form centralizes policy; typed-tree rewrite adds less machinery; Cranelift IR makes one engine canonical. Recommendation: new structured form.

  2. Should rustc's borrow check gate every corpus program?

    Gate blocks emitter drift; nightly reports later; none accepts blind spots. Recommendation: gate.

  3. Can safe Jet expose placement and lock-free control?

    Yes adds size_of, align_of, alignment, and atomic values; no keeps placement behind #Unsafe. Recommendation: yes, safe.

  4. In what order does a Float reduction add?

    Fixed pairwise locks parity and vectorizes; source order preserves sequence but can split tiers. Recommendation: fixed-width pairwise.

  5. May proven-independent loops accelerate themselves?

    Yes adds inspected, cost-gated transposition and parallelism with #Scalar and proposed #Serial; no leaves explicit layout only. Recommendation: yes.

Strongest unverified assumption: a Rust printer with no decisions of its own produces Rust that rustc optimizes as well as today's hand-shaped emission. The performance gate exists to test exactly that.
