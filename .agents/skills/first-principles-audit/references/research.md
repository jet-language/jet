# First-principles research

## Activate the scope

Before collecting evidence, write a finite scope ledger with the owner-named area, its related-concept closure, required dependencies, source sets, workloads, and output boundary. A concept is related when it shares the area's user job, value or data flow, declaration or representation mechanism, control or effect boundary, ratified decision, or relevant lexical space. The full corpus and silhouette mean every source in that closure, not every source in Jet.

Use the same closure for the parent audit and every child. A child returns to this audit owner, cannot start another audit, and cannot add a distant feature, workload, or source set. Record an out-of-scope opportunity for later instead of widening the run.

## Evidence slices

Submit independent read-only helpers when they reduce context. Use the owner guide's adaptive concurrency and adapter rules; do not choose a fixed worker count or model here. Each helper returns compact evidence with paths, line references, real syntax, probe output where needed, and decision IDs.

Cover these slices inside the declared closure:

1. **Current mechanisms.** Read the relevant specification, sema/compiler code, prelude, examples, and tests. Record how each mechanism is declared, checked, represented, erased, or reified.
2. **Adjacent planes.** Search related features that share the same job, data, control, effect, declaration, or dependency. Look for one job done twice under different names.
3. **Decision record.** Read ratified, ratified-but-unbuilt, declined, deferred, frozen, and superseded decisions before designing. Respect ratified law or name an amendment explicitly; open decisions can be resolved by the ballot slate.
4. **Silhouette.** Account for prior audits, open and frozen Tower cards, unpromoted ideas, phantom types, closed compiler tables, string-smuggled data, reserved names, missing code, and spec/code drift.
5. **Prior art.** Read peer-language evidence only when it explains the declared area. Record what unified or failed to unify, not a general popularity ranking.

Do not design during research. Probe the running binary for executable claims. Use the applicable sections of the standing lens for the declared area; do not force unrelated comparisons or runtime work. Missing or unavailable evidence is `unknown` with a reason, not a wider search.

## Research stop

Research is complete when each required ledger row has evidence or an honest `unknown`, every five-slice obligation above is accounted for, and the full corpus/silhouette has no unexamined source inside the declared closure. Do not keep searching for a stronger reader reaction; reactions are a later quality check, not a stop predicate.
