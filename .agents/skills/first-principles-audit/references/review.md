# First-principles review and closeout

Use a fresh context that assumes the design is wrong. Review the proposal, worked code, decision slate, and evidence together. Hunt for:

- an incomplete corpus or silhouette inside the declared closure;
- an unproved one idea, missing axes or law, or a kill-check that was skipped;
- a missing or hand-waved magic/control ladder;
- ceremony added to the common case;
- a default with no way to see it, no explicit replacement, or no project switch;
- silent ratified-law contradictions, fabricated or misused decision IDs, or frozen-wall reopening;
- wrong code-level claims, broken ratified syntax, invariant violations, strawman ballot options, or inconsistency between exsum, body, final vision, and ballots;
- an assertion that a diagnostic or mechanism dies without checking its load-bearing uses;
- an internals-only redesign, a lost capability, a new parallel mechanism, or poorer machine repair determinism;
- a syntax rethink that ignored open lexical space.

Fix every finding. Re-verify each materially fixed claim with appropriate evidence, and use a verifier different from the builder. Record the remaining unknowns rather than filling them with a stronger reaction or an invented result.

## Closeout checklist

Close only when the finite scope ledger is terminal, the required evidence has a source or `unknown`, the proposal's exsum, evidence table, worked redesign, ladder, final vision, unlocks, stays, decisions, and adoption boundary are present, and the authorized review has been recorded. Apply the shared finding-dispositions marker before publication. Report completion remains separate from implementation completion.

If the run commits, commit only its owned proposal, skill/docs, and Tower paths. Never commit another task's files. Keep card references out of commit messages because the githook rewrites `.tower` on `#N` mentions.
