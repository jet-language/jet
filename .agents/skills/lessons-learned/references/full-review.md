# Full lineage review

Load this reference when the owner asks for broad lineage, a cross-language
review, or coverage beyond one named peer or family. A focused request may use
the same evidence rules without sweeping unrelated families.

## Family scope

A broad request covers systems, managed, functional, scripting, config/OS, and
proof-oriented families. Name each family and each peer reviewed. If a family
has no suitable public evidence, mark it as unavailable with the reason; do not
replace the gap with a guess. Include the do-not-ballot list where Jet law
already answers the concern.

Use `.agents/skills/_shared/standing-lens.md` contextually. A full review
expands to the four questions, five agent-optimality quantities, relevant micro
categories, running-binary probes, and honesty rules. A focused review answers
only the questions and micro categories that bear on its named lineage scope,
and records non-applicable checks rather than doing unrelated work.

## Evidence and stop

For each peer and lesson, record exact source URL or repository, version or
commit, publication or retrieval date, and locator such as section, file:line,
timestamp, issue, or decision ID. Separate primary evidence, audience evidence,
local Jet evidence, and inference. Keep enough evidence to reproduce the claim.

Stop when every named peer/family has the requested evidence, Jet state, failure
mode, guard, and uncertainty recorded. A broad review stops only after every
named family is covered or marked unavailable. Do not extend the review because
new interesting peers appear outside the declared scope.

## Evidence-weighted halves

Report both **Avoid** and **Beat** where evidence supports them. Do not force
equal counts or equal depth. Give more space to the side with stronger,
more consequential evidence, and state when one side has only weak or no
support.

- **Avoid**: peer failure → Jet risk → guard. Include structural immunity once
  when it is a design asset.
- **Beat**: peer strength → mechanism → Jet state (`shipped`,
  `ratified-but-unbuilt`, or `absent`). Rank by how categorical and well-supported
  the win is. State what the peer would have to change to match Jet, including
  when it could copy the idea without breaking its model.

Do not turn the report into a ballot, card, or implementation change.
