# Elegance dive

Redesign the owner-chosen target until it either clicks or proves it cannot.

## Close the target

Record the target and its closure: every part of the target, plus every form in other layers that rhymes with it or must change in the same cutover. Inventory the closure completely from executable homes, read its ratified decisions and `Syntax.rs` decision IDs, and read the relevant Tower decisions and cards read-only. Record an opportunity outside the closure in one line instead of widening the dive.

## Find the law

Draft at least two candidate laws, each in one sentence. For each law, list the parts it predicts, the exceptions it leaves, and the parts it cannot place. Choose the law that predicts the most with the fewest exceptions, and keep the losing laws with the reason they lost. When no law places most of the target, report that result instead of forcing one.

Then shape the end state so it obeys the chosen law: regroup, renumber, rename, merge, or delete parts. Every existing capability must survive as an instance of the law or as a documented wall. Show the beginner path and the expert path for each changed part. The beginner path must not gain ceremony, and expert control must stay reachable.

## Prove the click

Run the prediction test before building the showcase:

1. Pick at least five held-out forms from the end state, or every form if there are fewer than five. Choose forms the law should predict, including at least one from each rhyming layer, and choose them before writing the showcase.
2. Give a fresh-context helper only the law sentence and one question per form, such as "What code range holds type errors?" or "Which command lists installed packages?"
3. Score each answer as **hit** (exactly right), **near** (right after one extra fact, which you name), or **miss**.

Report the score and every near fact. A law that misses more forms than it hits is not a click: narrow it or drop it. Every near fact is an exception and goes into the area verdict.

Then build the showcase: real before/after surfaces for the same job, side by side, plus the one sentence the reader should think when they see the after column. Mark every line that is not ratified as `proposed`.

## Measure the area

Recount parts and exceptions for the end state with the same method as the census. Report before, after, and the net change for parts and for exceptions separately. A click that adds a part is allowed; state what it costs.

## Kill-check and amendments

Run the kill-check from [`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md#verdicts) on every slice and record what was killed or narrowed. Name every ratified rule, decision ID, or stability rule that the end state amends. List the migration surface of the one cutover, including registries, raise sites, snapshots, examples, goldens, generated pages, and specs, as a list rather than as work. Turn each owner-only choice into a proposed ballot title with its options.

## Dive stop

The dive is complete when the closure inventory is complete; the chosen law and the rejected laws are recorded; the prediction test has a score from a fresh helper; the showcase, area recount, kill-check, amendments, migration surface, and proposed ballot titles are present; and every unverified claim is marked `unknown`.
