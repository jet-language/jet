# Elegance report, review, and publication

Load the `simple` skill and write in direct, plain prose. Do not hard-wrap paragraphs. Let tables, code, and before/after pairs carry each claim.

## Shape

1. **Summary.** The headline click in one sentence, its two verdicts, the strongest existing click worth celebrating, and the biggest false rhyme.
2. **Census.** The five-row ledger, the rhyme map, and the celebrate list.
3. **Dive.** The closure, chosen and rejected laws, prediction-test score with near facts, showcase, area recount, kill-check result, amendments, and migration surface. For a census-only run, give the ranked candidates instead.
4. **Owner decisions.** Proposed ballot titles with their options. None is created.
5. **Unknowns.** Each missing count or unproven claim, with the reason.
6. **Finding dispositions.** The marker from [`audit-dispositions.md`](../../_shared/audit-dispositions.md).

## Review

Give the draft to a fresh-context reviewer who assumes the design is ugly and the counts are flattering. The reviewer hunts for:

- a miscounted ledger, or counts taken from prose instead of executable homes;
- an exception hidden inside a law's wording, or a law that needs "except" to state;
- held-out forms chosen to flatter the law, or a prediction test scored by its author;
- a rhyme that is decoration, or a false rhyme presented as a click;
- merged verdicts, or an area gain that hides a lost capability;
- a skipped kill-check, added beginner ceremony, or a lost expert path;
- an amended rule or decision ID that goes unnamed, or a fabricated one.

Fix every finding and re-check each fixed claim. Record whatever remains unknown.

## Publish

Publish once, after the review, with the non-serve CLI form from `audit-dispositions.md`, using section `audits` and id `elegance-audit-YYYY-MM-DD`. This method permits a same-day `docs update`. Read the published document back. Create no cards, decisions, or ballots. If the run commits, commit only the report path.

## Report stop

The report is complete when every section is present, the review findings are fixed or recorded as unknown, the disposition marker is complete, and the published report has been read back.
