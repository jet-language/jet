# Capture a discussion thread

Treat the thread as both body and audience. Capture it whole when bounded. If it
is not bounded, capture top-level posts plus highest-signal subthreads and
record what was skipped.

Preserve post or comment IDs, timestamps, linked sources, and reply boundaries.
Separate the author's claim, corrections, workarounds, and preference. Do not
use reactions as evidence of technical truth.
