Jet's ranked priorities: safety first, beginner experience second, expert control third. No reason to reach for another language — jack-of-all-trades, master-of-ALL.

The front end owns all semantics and every error message. rustc is a hidden verifier and optimizer, never a user-facing tool.

See [[syntax-decisions]] for the concrete syntax ratification record.
