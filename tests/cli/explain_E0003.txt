E0003

What this means:
  statement parse error; expected a current statement form

Why Jet enforces it:
  this position accepts only Jet's current statement grammar; retired foreign loop words are not statement keywords

How to fix it:
  write the current form named by the diagnostic, such as `loop`, `next`, `@if`, or a binding

Example:
  fn save(path: String) ? IOError {
      print(path)
  }

This explanation comes from jet's diagnostics reference.
