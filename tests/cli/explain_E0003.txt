E0003

What this means:
  expected X, found Y, or a retired surface form

Why Jet enforces it:
  the parser knows the one current form for this declaration

How to fix it:
  write the form named by the diagnostic, such as `fn save(path: String) ? IOError`

Example:
  fn save(path: String) ? IOError {
      print(path)
  }

This explanation comes from jet's diagnostics reference.
