E1803

What this means:
  Application authority is undecided or denies a required effect; the report names `required_effects`, `granted_effects`, `denied_effects`, and `authority` separately.

Why Jet enforces it:
  The effect projection distinguishes sema requirements from policy grants, denials, and the policy identity; execution stops before host state changes.

How to fix it:
  Declare the effect in `authority.holds.allow`, deny it deliberately, or approve the exact operation once or for the project in an interactive terminal.

This explanation comes from jet's diagnostics reference.
