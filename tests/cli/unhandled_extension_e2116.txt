Error [jet.UnhandledSourceExtension]: `jet run` doesn't handle `.txt` files like `notes.txt`.
 Why: Jet tells a file's language by its extension, and `jet run` takes `.jet`, `.c`, `.cc`, `.cpp` and `.cxx`.
 Fix: Give `jet run` a `.jet` file; `jet run` and `jet build` also compile C (`.c`) and C++ (`.cc`, `.cpp`, `.cxx`).
More: jet-lang.dev/e/E2116
