E3012

What this means:
  Stack overflow in `{fn_name}`

Why Jet enforces it:
  The call stack kept growing without reaching a safe return — with Jet file, line, and source context

How to fix it:
  End the recursion or make progress toward a base case, or give deep work more stack with `task.group deep(stack: bytes)`.

This explanation comes from jet's diagnostics reference.
