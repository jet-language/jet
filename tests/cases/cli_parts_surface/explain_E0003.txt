E0003

What this means:
  Expected a current statement form, found an unsupported or incomplete form

Why Jet enforces it:
  This position accepts only a complete statement from Jet's current grammar

How to fix it:
  Write the complete statement form named by the diagnostic

Example:
  save: fn(path: String) IOError! {
      print(path)
  }

This explanation comes from jet's diagnostics reference.
