E3003

What this means:
  `deadline exceeded while waiting in {wait_kind}`.

Why Jet enforces it:
  A wait/IO point observed an active `#Context(deadline: …)` budget and the remaining time reached zero before the operation completed.

How to fix it:
  Raise the deadline budget, shorten the work before the wait point, or remove/adjust the ambient deadline for this scope.

This explanation comes from jet's diagnostics reference.
