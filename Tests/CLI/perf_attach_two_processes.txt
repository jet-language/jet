Error: Jet.AttachWhichProcess: `jet perf attach` was given two process ids, `4242` and `4343`
 Why: One attach profiles one process, named by one process id, so jet won't guess which process you mean.
 Fix: Attach to one of them at a time: `jet perf attach 4242 --source <file.jet>`
More: jet-lang.dev/e/Jet.AttachWhichProcess
